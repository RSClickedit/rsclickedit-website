Hi, I'm Ronald.

I started RS Clicked It to give Philadelphia schools, organizations and families creative work they're proud to share.

Some projects last a single evening, like a prom send-off a family will look back on for years. Others run all year, like a school that wants new families to feel what its classrooms are really like.

Either way, the approach is the same: plan it with you, capture it with care and deliver photos, films and designs that are ready to use. Strategy and production live under one roof, so nothing gets lost between the idea and the final file.
