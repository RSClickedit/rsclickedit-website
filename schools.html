How it works

Three steps, built around your calendar.

  1. Plan

    We meet with your team to set goals, a schedule and a shot list that fits your school day.

  2. Capture

    We photograph and film with as little disruption to classes as possible, following your school's media consent rules.

  3. Deliver

    You get edited photos, films and graphics, ready for your website, social media and families.
